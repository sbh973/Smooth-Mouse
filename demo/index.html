    Gemini only sees button labels and small icon images, never what you type. This works on every website: open the Smooth Mouse popup to see a page's risky buttons.

    Things to try

    • Risky click: with the shaky hand on, click Send or the 🗑 button. Smooth Mouse asks first.
    • Quick taps: with "Ignore quick clicks" on, a fast tap is ignored; hold until the ring turns teal.
    • Repeated keys: type "hhello" in the message and get "hello".
    • Magnet: move near the tiny ⚙ or B button; it lights up and catches your click.
